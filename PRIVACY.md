# AI Notebook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

AI Notebook is a browser extension that saves ChatGPT conversations you choose as study notebooks.

## What the extension accesses

- The text of ChatGPT conversations on chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com to capture or update notebooks and save highlights you choose. Active notebooks can capture new replies automatically until you pause updates or finish the notebook.
- The highlights, notes, tags, bookmarks and settings you create.

## Where your data is stored

All data is stored locally in your browser on your own device. AI Notebook has no server, no account system and no analytics. The extension does not send your notebook data to the developer or to any third party.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, sell or share your data.
- We do not track your browsing or activity.
- We do not use your data for advertising or any purpose other than the extension's features.

## Deleting your data

You can remove highlights inside the extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes its locally stored browser data. Exported PDFs remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

## Donations

The extension contains an optional link to Ko-fi (ko-fi.com). Opening it is your choice and is subject to Ko-fi's own privacy policy.
